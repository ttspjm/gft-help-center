1 Step Models

  • 1 STEP MODEL

    The 1-Step Model features a single Evaluation Phase, offering the fastest path to a simulated funded account. Achieve the profit target while staying within the risk parameters to become a Funded GOAT Trader.

2 Step Models

  • 2-Step PRO Model

    The 2-Step PRO Model evaluates your trading in two phases. If you successfully complete Step 1 and Step 2 without breaking any rules, you qualify for a Simulated Funded Account and become a Funded GOAT Trader (subject to review).

  • 2-Step Standard

    The 2-Step Standard Model is our most popular evaluation, offering balanced risk limits and a clear path to becoming a Funded GOAT Trader. Complete both evaluation phases to unlock your simulated funded account.

  • 2 Step GOAT Model

    The 2-Step Model is designed to test and refine your trading skills in two phases. Successfully completing both phases without violating any trading rules will qualify you for a Simulated funded account.

3 Step Models

  • 3 STEP MODEL

    The 3-Step Model is designed for traders who prefer a lower profit target across multiple phases to reach a simulated funded account. Complete all three evaluation phases to unlock your account.
