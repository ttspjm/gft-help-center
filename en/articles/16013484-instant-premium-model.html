Instant Premium Model

Instant Premium offers instant access to a funded trading account, governed by clear rules for risk and profit management, along with a set daily drawdown limit for more structured risk control.

  • Access Type: Immediate Funded
  • Daily Drawdown: 3%
  • Max Overall Loss: 6% (Intraday Trailing)
  • Minimum Profitable Days: 5 (at 0.5%)
  • Floating Loss Rule: 1.5% Floating Loss (1% for accounts purchased from September 2nd, 2026)
  • Consistency Rule: NO

Hard breach are serious violations to our established rules. A hard breach happens when a trader exceeds the maximum daily loss or total drawdown limit, resulting in the immediate closure of the account.

1. Max Daily Drawdown – 3%

What does this mean?

  • The maximum daily loss limit is 3% of your daily starting balance.

How is it calculated?

  • The daily drawdown is calculated daily at 5 PM EST based on your account balance or equity. Your equity must not drop by more than 3% of your initial account balance in a single trading day.
Worked example
For a $100,000 account, the daily drawdown limit is $3,000. If your account balance or equity drops below $97,000, you will exceed your daily loss limit.

2. Max Total Drawdown – 6% (Intraday Trailing)

What does this mean?

  • The maximum total drawdown for Instant Premium accounts is a trailing drawdown set at 6% of the equity value. This means that your maximum loss limit adjusts based on your account’s performance. If your equity increases, your maximum drawdown limit also moves up. However, it does not decrease if your equity drops, ensuring a dynamic but protective risk management system.
Worked example
For a $100,000 account:
If your account reaches $104,000, your new trailing drawdown limit becomes $97,760 (6% below your account’s equity)
If your account then drops to $103,000, your new trailing drawdown limit will still be $97,760.
Your drawdown limit follows your Equity value and remains 6% of that value.

N.B - The Maximum Loss Limit resets after each payout.

Understanding the 1.5% Max Floating Loss Rule

If your floating (unrealized) loss reaches -1.5% of your account balance at any moment, the account will be permanently closed. This rule enforces strict risk management and prevents excessive losses.

For example, on a $50,000 account, if your combined open trade losses reach -$750 (-1.5%), the account will be immediately closed.

Note - The 1.5% Floating Loss Rule is an additional risk management rule and applies independently of the 3% Daily Drawdown and 6% Trailing End-of-Day Maximum Drawdown. All three rules must be respected at all times.

Exception: For all challenges purchased from September 2nd, 2026 onwards, the Floating Loss hard breach threshold is -1% instead of -1.5%. The example above scales accordingly — on a $50,000 account, this means the account is closed once combined open trade losses reach -$500 (-1%).

Minimum profitable day requirement for Instant Premium accounts

Traders on Instant Premium accounts must complete at least 5 profitable days before requesting a reward. These days do not have to be consecutive. To count as a minimum profitable day, the day must close with a profit of at least 0.5% of the initial balance, net of fees and commissions.

Consistency Rule

Instant Premium accounts do not have a consistency rule. There is no requirement for profits to be spread evenly across trading days. You are free to generate profits however they come, without needing to balance out a high-profit day.

Rewards & Profit Split

Once you are a Funded GOAT Trader, the following rules apply:

Profit Split: 80% (Traders keep 80% of their profit).

Payout Cycle: Every 10 days. For accounts purchased from September 21st, 2026 onwards, the payout cycle is bi-weekly (every 14 days).
