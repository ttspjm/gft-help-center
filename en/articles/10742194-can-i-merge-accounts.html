Can I merge accounts?

Updated 30 August 2026

Merging combines your simulated Funded Accounts into a single account with a larger balance, so you can trade with more capital under one account. Here's everything you need to know about when it's possible.

Which accounts can be merged?

Only simulated Funded Accounts that came from an Evaluation model can be merged.

These can never be merged:

  • Evaluation accounts, at any stage.
  • Instant Funding accounts of any kind, including Instant GOAT, Instant PRO, Instant HERO, Instant Premium and Instant Standard. An Instant account cannot be merged with another Instant account, and it cannot be merged with an account from any other model.

Conditions for merging

You can merge your eligible Funded Accounts when all of the following are true across every account you want to merge:

  • No trades placed - each account must still be at its initial balance. This means the account is either brand new (untraded) or has just been reset following a payout.
  • Same account type - all accounts must be the same type.
  • Same profit split - all accounts must share the same profit split.
  • Same add-ons - all accounts must have the same add-ons, or none of them have any add-ons.

Do the account sizes have to match?

No. Account sizes do not need to be the same. For example, a $5,000 account and a $50,000 account can be merged into a single $55,000 account, as long as all the conditions above are met.

Is there a limit?

The only cap is a combined total of $400,000, our permitted maximum allocation per trader. You can merge as many accounts as you like, as long as their balances add up to no more than that.

How do I request a merge?

If your accounts meet all the conditions above, reach out to our support team and we'll help you complete the merge.
