3 STEP MODEL

The 3-Step Model is designed for traders who prefer a lower profit target across multiple phases to reach a simulated funded account. Complete all three evaluation phases to unlock your account.

Values by phase: Step 1 (Evaluation) / Step 2 (Evaluation) / Step 3 (Evaluation) / Step 4 (Funded)

  • Profit Target: 6% / 6% / 6% / None
  • Daily Drawdown: 4% / 4% / 4% / 4%
  • Max Total Loss: 8% (Static) / 8% (Static) / 8% (Static) / 8% (Static)
  • Minimum Profitable Days: No / No / No / 3 Days (per payout); 4 Days for accounts purchased from 27 July 2026 (per payout)
  • Daily Profit Cap: No Limit / No Limit / No Limit / $3,000
  • Consistency Rule: No / No / No / No

Step 1 (Evaluation)

To pass Step 1, you must:

  • Achieve a 6% profit target.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits. After passing Step 1, you automatically proceed to Step 2.

Step 2 (Evaluation)

To pass Step 2, you must:

  • Achieve a 6% profit target.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits. After passing Step 2, you automatically proceed to Step 3.

Step 3 (Evaluation)

To pass Step 3, you must:

  • Achieve a 6% profit target.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits. After passing Step 3, your entire evaluation is reviewed. If successful, you become a Funded GOAT Trader.

Step 4 (Funded)

  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits including goat guard (What Is Goat Guard).
  • Complete at least 3 profitable days, each with a minimum of 0.5% to be eligible for a Payout (Resets after each payout)

Exception: For accounts purchased on or after July 27th, 2026, the Funded stage requires 4 profitable days, each with a minimum of 0.5% profit to be eligible for a payout, instead of the standard 3-day requirement.

1. Daily Drawdown Limit 4%

How is it calculated?

  • At 5:00 PM EST, we check the higher value between the Account Balance or Account Equity.
  • Subtract 4% of that value from that value to set the Daily Drawdown Threshold for the next trading day.
Worked example
Account Balance$105,000
Account Equity$107,000 (Higher)
Threshold($107,000 × 4%) = $4,280
$107,000 - $4,280$102,720
Your balance/equity must stay above $102,720 for that day.

2. Maximum Loss Limit (Hard Breach) — 8% (Static)

This is your Absolute Floor. Your account equity or balance must never drop below 92% of your starting capital.

How is it calculated?

  • Maximum Loss = Initial Account Size − 8%

Worked example
Account Size$100,000
$100,000 X 8%$8,000
$100,000 - $8,000$92,000.
Your Absolute Floor: Your account equity/balance must never drop below $92,000.

Rewards & Profit Split

Once you are a Funded GOAT Trader, the following rules apply:

Profit Split: 80% (Traders keep 80% of their profit).

Payout Cycle: Bi-weekly (Every 14 days).

Daily Profit Cap: Funded accounts have a $3,000 daily profit limit.

*Note: Profits exceeding this are deducted but do NOT result in an account breach.

Initial 2 Payout Caps: For the first two payouts, withdrawals are capped at 6% of the initial account size or $10,000 (whichever is lower). This restriction is removed after the 2nd successful payout.

Leverage Settings

To maintain a professional risk environment, leverage is adjusted between phases:

  • Evaluation Phases: Forex (1:100), Indices/Commodities (1:20), Crypto (1:2).
  • Funded Phase: Forex (1:50), Indices/Commodities (1:10), Crypto (1:2).
