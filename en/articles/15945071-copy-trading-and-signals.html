Copy trading and signals

Updated 12 October 2026

Signal services and copying trades from other people are not permitted on GOAT Funded Trader accounts.

Copying trades from signal providers, trading groups, third-party services, or other traders is considered a prohibited trading practice and may result in account termination.

Copy trading is allowed only through the GOAT Trade Copier.
You can copy trades between your own Goat Funded Trader accounts, including from one evaluation account to another and between evaluation and funded accounts in either direction.
You can also copy trades from your own external trading account into your Goat Funded Trader accounts. In both cases you must use the GOAT Trade Copier, which you can find in the Trader Dashboard. It works on MT5, cTrader, Match-Trader and TradeLocker. This rule applies from October 12, 2026.

Any other trade copier, copy trading tool or copier EA is not allowed. Using one is a prohibited trading practice and may result in account termination.

All trading decisions must be made independently by the account holder. Traders are expected to manage their own positions and execute trades based on their own analysis and judgment.

If you are unsure whether a particular service or trading method is permitted, please contact our Support team before using it.
