Step 1 (Evaluation)

To pass Step 1, you must:

  • Achieve a 3% profit target.
  • Complete at least 5 profitable days.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits.

After passing Step 1, your full evaluation will be reviewed.
If approved, you will become a Funded GOAT Trader.

Step 2 (Funded)

Once funded, you must:

  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits.
  • Respect the 15% consistency rule during each payout period.
  • Complete at least 5 profitable days to be eligible for a Payout (Resets after each payout)

What Is A Minimum Profitable Day?

  • A minimum profitable day is a day on which you close at least one position and your profit for that day is at least 0.5% of the initial account balance, net of fees and commissions.

1. Daily Drawdown Limit – 3%

What does this mean?

  • The maximum daily loss limit is 3% of your starting balance for the day.

How is it calculated?

  • The daily drawdown is calculated daily at 5 PM EST based on your account balance or equity.
  • At the reset, we take the higher of your balance and equity and set your daily limit 3% below it. The limit stays the same until the next reset.
Worked example
Higher of balance and equity at the reset$104,000
Daily Loss Limit$104,000 X 3% = $3,120
$104,000 - $3,120$100,880
Your balance/equity must stay above $100,880 for that day.

2. Max Overall Loss – 5% (Trailing)

What does this mean?

  • The maximum overall loss is a fixed amount equal to 5% of your initial account balance, which is $5,000 on a $100,000 account.
  • This is a trailing drawdown, which means the drawdown threshold moves up as your equity increases, but it does not move back down if your equity later drops.

How is it calculated?

  • Your limit sits $5,000 below the highest balance or equity your account has reached. It moves up as soon as you reach a new high and never moves down.
Worked example
For A $100,000 account:
If your account reaches $105,000, your new trailing drawdown limit becomes $100,000 ($5,000 below $105,000).
If your account then drops to $103,000, your trailing drawdown limit will remain at $100,000.

Floating Loss Limit (2%)

If the combined floating (unrealised) loss of all your open trades reaches -2% of your initial account balance at any moment, the account is permanently closed. This is not a per-trade limit. Every open position counts toward the 2%, so the account can close even when no single trade is down 2%.

Worked example
For a $50,000 account, if your combined open trade losses reach -$1,000 (-2%), the account will be immediately closed

Consistency Rule

The consistency rule ensures steady and sustainable profits. No single trading day can account for more than 15% of your total profits during a payout period.

Worked example
If you make $1,500 in one trading day, your total profits must reach at least $10,000 to be eligible to request a payout.
You cannot request a payout until the highest profit day is no more than 15% of the total profits for the period.

What Happens if I Violate the 15% Rule?

Will my account be terminated?

  • No, violating the 15% rule will not result in account termination. However, you will not be able to request a payout until your highest profit trading day is no more than 15% of total profits.

What should I do next?

  • Continue trading and generating profits until your highest profit day is at or below the 15% threshold. Once this happens, you will be eligible to request a payout again.

N.B - The consistency rule applies to funded accounts only and not evaluation accounts.
