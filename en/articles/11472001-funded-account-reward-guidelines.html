Funded Account Reward Guidelines

Updated 5 October 2026

At Goat Funded Trader, we prioritize a fair and secure trading environment. To maintain stability, we have implemented the following rules for funded accounts:

Daily Profit Limit

  • Traders on funded accounts can generate a maximum profit of $3,000 per trading day.
  • Any profits exceeding this limit will be deducted from the account.
  • This is not considered a breach; the deduction is only to maintain a controlled and sustainable trading system.

Profit Withdrawal Limits for Initial Rewards

  • For the first two reward requests, traders can withdraw a maximum of 6% of the initial account balance or $10,000, whichever is lower. For $5K Two-Step accounts (Goat and Standard) purchased from 25 July 2026 onward, the cap is 4% of the initial account balance. The same 4% cap applies to $5K Instant HERO accounts purchased from 6 October 2026 onward. This cap does not apply to Goat 1$ accounts.
  • Any profits beyond this limit will be deducted from the account balance.
  • After the first two rewards, this restriction is removed, and there are no further withdrawal limits.

Why Are These Limits Necessary?

These limits help create a stable and secure trading environment while allowing us to properly assess each trader’s performance. The period between purchasing the program and receiving the first rewards is often too short for a full evaluation of trading behavior. By implementing these temporary restrictions, we ensure fairness, sustainability, and long-term opportunities for all traders.

If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact our support team. We are committed to transparency and efficiency in serving our traders.
