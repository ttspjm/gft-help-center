Reward on Demand Eligibility Criteria

Updated 27 July 2026

First Reward on Demand is available as an add-on for all our 1-Step, 2-Steps, 3-Steps and Goat Blitz challenges.

To qualify for a Reward on Demand while trading on a funded account, traders must meet the following criteria:

  1. Minimum Profit & Trading Days: Traders must achieve a minimum profit of 3% in their account over at least 3 trading days.
  2. Minimum Daily Profit: Traders must generate at least 0.5% profit per day on a minimum of 3 trading days.

For the first Reward on Demand, a 40% profit split will be applied. After this, traders become eligible for subsequent rewards every 14 or 30 days, at the usual full 80% profit split.

Please note that failing to meet any of the above criteria will result in ineligibility for Reward on Demand. Ensure compliance with these requirements to take advantage of expedited reward processing.

Please note that The 100% Profit Split add-on does not change the Reward on Demand profit split from 40% on first payout. Regardless of add-ons purchased, the first Reward on Demand will always be paid at a 40% profit split.
