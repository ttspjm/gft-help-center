  • Hard breaches are serious violations to our established rules. A hard breach happens when a trader exceeds the maximum daily loss or total drawdown limit, resulting in the immediate closure of the account.

1. Max Daily Drawdown – 3% (Static)

  • What does this mean?

    The maximum daily loss limit is 3% of your initial account balance/Equity (the highest between the two at market rollover time).

  • How is it calculated?

    The daily drawdown is calculated daily at 5 PM EST based on your account balance or equity. Your equity must not drop by more than 3% of your initial account balance in a single trading day.
Worked example
For a fresh new $1,000 account, the daily drawdown limit is $30. If your account balance or equity drops below $970, you will exceed your daily loss limit.

2. Max Total Drawdown – 6% (Trailing)

  • What does this mean?

    The maximum total drawdown for Goat 1$ accounts is a trailing drawdown set at 6% of the equity value at the end of each trading day. This means that your maximum loss limit adjusts based on your account’s performance at the end of each day. If your equity increases, your maximum drawdown limit also moves up. However, it does not decrease if your equity drops, ensuring a dynamic but protective risk management system.
Worked example
For a $1,000 account:
If your account reaches $1,040 at the end of a trading day, your new trailing drawdown limit becomes $977.60 (6% below your account’s equity).
If your account then drops to $1,030, your new trailing drawdown limit will still be $977.60.
Your drawdown limit follows your equity value at the end of each trading day and remains 6% of that value.

Understanding the Maximum Loss Per Trade Rule

If your floating profit and loss (PnL) drops below -2% of your account balance at any moment, the account will be permanently closed. This rule enforces strict risk management and prevents excessive losses.

For example, on a $1,000 account, if your combined open trade losses reach -$20 (-2%), the account will be immediately closed.

Minimum profitable day requirement for Instant Goat 1$ accounts

Traders on Instant Goat accounts must complete at least 3 profitable days before requesting a reward. These days do not have to be consecutive. To count as a minimum profitable day, the day must close with a profit of at least 0.5% of the initial balance, net of fees and commissions.

Consistency Rule

  • What is it?

    The consistency rule ensures steady and sustainable profits over time. It states that no single trading day can account for more than 15% of your total profits during a payout period.
Worked example
If you make $15 in one trading day, your total profits must reach at least $100 to be eligible to request a payout. You cannot request a payout until the highest profit day is no more than 15% of the total profits for the period.

What Happens if I Violate the 15% Rule?

  • Will my account be terminated?
    No, violating the 15% rule will not result in account termination. However, you will be unable to request a payout until your highest profit trading day is no more than 15% of your total profits.
  • What should I do next?
    Continue trading and generating profits until your highest profit day is at or below the 15% threshold. Once this happens, you will be eligible to request a payout again.

Rewards

Traders receive an 80% profit split as standard.

Example:

If a trader earns a profit of $100 and requests a payout, they will receive $80 as their total payout.

Reward Cycle

We offer bi-weekly payouts, allowing traders to withdraw their profits every 14 days. Simply request a reward, and your earnings will be processed accordingly.

What is the minimum profit I can withdraw with the Goat 1$ plan?

The minimum withdrawal amount for the Goat $1 account is $100. This means you must accumulate at least $100 in profit before you can request a withdrawal. Withdrawals below this threshold will not be processed.

Note: For accounts purchased from July 30th, 2026 onward, the minimum payout request amount is $100 — requests below this amount will not be accepted.

What is the maximum profit I can withdraw with the Goat 1$ plan?

The maximum withdraw-able profit for the Goat 1$ account is capped at $100 for the entire life of the account. This means that the total profit you can withdraw is limited to 10% of the initial account balance.

What account sizes are available for the Goat 1$ plan?

The only available account size for the Goat 1$ plan is $1,000, and it costs just $1 to get started.

Does the Goat 1$ account expire?

Yes, the Goat 1$ account will automatically expire 28 days after the account is activated. After 28 days, the account will be blocked and you will not be able to trade on it or request withdrawals.

Can I buy multiple Goat 1$ accounts?

No. Each user is entitled to purchase only one Goat 1$ account.

Can I merge a Goat 1$ account?

No. Goat 1$ is an Instant Funding account, and Instant Funding accounts cannot be merged. This applies to merges with another Instant account and to merges with an account from any other model, whether the account is new, traded or freshly reset after a reward.

Each user is limited to one Goat 1$ account, so there is also no second Goat 1$ account to merge it with.
