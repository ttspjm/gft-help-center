How does the compensation structure work in the Affiliate program?

Updated 3 July 2026

Our Affiliate Program offers a competitive and rewarding compensation structure designed to grow with you as you refer more clients. Here's a detailed breakdown of the new tiers and bonuses:

Commission Tiers

  • Starting Tier: Begin earning 10% commission on all referred client activity from day one.
  • Tier Two: Once you reach 50 referrals, your commission increases to 12%.
  • Tier Three: At 150 referrals, your commission jumps to 15%.

Cookie Tracking

We use a 14-day cookie tracking window. Any sale generated within 14 days of a referral click will count toward your commissions.

Sub-Affiliate Commissions

Affiliates can earn a fixed 3.5% commission on sub-affiliate activity. This applies to one level with unlimited sub-affiliates.

GFT Partner Perks (500+ Referrals)

Becoming a GFT Partner unlocks premium benefits including:

  • Personalized support from our team
  • Exclusive gifts or merchandise
  • Access to advanced affiliate resources

Please note that the minimum amount for an affiliate commissions payout request is $100.
