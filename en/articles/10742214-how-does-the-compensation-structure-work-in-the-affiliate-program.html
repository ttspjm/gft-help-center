How does the compensation structure work in the Affiliate program?

Updated 13 November 2026

You earn commission on every sale made through your affiliate link. You also earn 5% of the sales made by affiliates you invite, called sub-affiliates.

Your commission

  • 12% on your first 100 sales.
  • 15% on every sale after your 100th.

There is no separate rate for your first sale.

Sub-affiliate commission

Invite other creators, influencers or traders to join the affiliate program with your invite link. Everyone who joins through it becomes your sub-affiliate, and you earn 5% of the sales they make. This comes on top of the commission on your own sales.

There is no limit on how many sub-affiliates you can invite. The 5% covers one level only, so you don't earn on affiliates that your sub-affiliates invite.

Your invite link is in the Affiliate section of your dashboard.

Example

Say you invite 10 creators and each of them makes $2,000 in sales this month. That's $20,000 in total, and 5% of it is $1,000 for you, without selling anything extra yourself.

If your sub-affiliates grow and sell more, your 5% grows with them.

When you can request your commission

You can request a commission 30 days after it's credited to your account. Each commission has its own 30 days. For example, commission credited on 13 November can be requested from 13 December.

The minimum payout request is $100. For more detail, see When can I withdraw my affiliate commission?

Cookie tracking

We use a 14-day cookie. If someone clicks your link and buys within 14 days, the sale counts toward your commission.

GFT Partner perks (500+ referrals)

Affiliates with 500 or more referrals become GFT Partners and get:

  • Personal support from our team
  • Exclusive gifts or merchandise
  • Access to advanced affiliate resources

Paid ads

Running paid ads to earn commission is not allowed. See Can I use paid ads to earn affiliate commissions?
