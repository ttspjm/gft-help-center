How does the commission work for different trading instruments?

Updated 27 July 2026

For MT5, Match-Trade and TradeLocker, our commission structure involves a flat fee of $0 per lot for cryptocurrencies, indices, commodities and stocks, $5 for FX pairs and metals with raw spread on all assets.
