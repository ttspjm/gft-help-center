Is News Trading allowed?

Updated 10 November 2026

Can I trade during news events with Goat Funded Trader?

Yes. Trading during news events is allowed and does not break any Goat Funded Trader rules.

Is there a limit on profit from trades around high-impact news?

Yes. A trade opened or closed within 5 minutes before or after a high-impact news release (marked with a red folder on ForexFactory.com or Myfxbook.com) has a maximum profit per trade. The maximum depends on when you purchased the account:

  • Purchased on or after 10 November 2026: 3% of the account's initial balance per trade
  • Purchased before 10 November 2026: 1% of the account's initial balance per trade

This includes trades closed manually or automatically through:

  • Stop Loss
  • Take Profit
  • Pending Orders

Which limit applies to my account?

The purchase date decides it, and nothing else. Accounts purchased before 10 November 2026 keep the 1% limit. They do not move to 3%.

A funded account follows the purchase date of the challenge it came from. If you bought the challenge before 10 November 2026 and pass after that date, your funded account stays at 1%. If you reset an account that was purchased before 10 November 2026, it also stays at 1%, even if the reset happens after that date.

Does this policy apply to both the challenge and funded phases?

Yes. Both limits apply to the challenge phase and the funded phase.

What happens if a trade makes more than the limit?

The limit applies to each trade separately. If a single trade in the news window makes more than your limit, only the profit above the limit is removed. You keep the profit up to the limit.

Example on a $100,000 account: a trade opened inside the 5-minute news window closes with $4,500 profit.

  • Purchased on or after 10 November 2026: the limit is $3,000. You keep $3,000 and $1,500 is removed.
  • Purchased before 10 November 2026: the limit is $1,000. You keep $1,000 and $3,500 is removed.

This is not a breach and there is no penalty on the account. The deduction usually happens during account reviews.
