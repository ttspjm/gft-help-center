How can I find my Order ID?

Updated 27 July 2026

Your Order ID is automatically generated once your purchase is successfully completed.

Order Confirmation Email

  1. After completing your purchase, you will receive an Order Confirmation email sent to the email address used at checkout just as below.
  2. Open the email and scroll to the Order Summary section.
  3. Your Order ID is displayed just below the order summary as circled below.

If you don’t see the email, please check your Spam / Promotions folder.

Still can’t find your Order ID?

If you are unable to locate your Order ID, please contact our support team and provide the following details:

  • Email address used for the purchase
  • Approximate date of purchase
  • Payment method used
  • Screenshot or proof of payment

Providing this information will help us locate your order faster and assist you efficiently.
