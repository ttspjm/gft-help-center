1 STEP MODEL

The 1-Step Model features a single Evaluation Phase, offering the fastest path to a simulated funded account. Achieve the profit target while staying within the risk parameters to become a Funded GOAT Trader.

  • Max Overall Loss: 6% (Static) / 6% (Static)
  • Minimum Profitable Days: 3 Days / 4 Days (per payout)
  • Daily Profit Cap: No Limit / $3,000
  • Consistency Rule: No / No

Step 1 (Evaluation)

To pass Step 1, you must:

  • Achieve a 10% profit target.
  • Complete at least 3 profitable days.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits. After passing Step 1, your entire evaluation is reviewed. If successful, you become a Funded GOAT Trader.

Step 2 (Funded)

  • Complete at least 4 profitable days each with a minimum of 0.5% to be eligible for a Payout (Resets after each payout)
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits including goat guard (What Is Goat Guard).

What Is A Minimum Profitable Day?

  • A minimum profitable day is a day on which you close at least one position and your profit for that day is at least 0.5% of the initial account balance, net of fees and commissions.

Account Violations (Hard Breach)

Violation of these rules results in immediate account closure.

1. Daily Drawdown Limit

How is it calculated?

  • At 5:00 PM EST, we check the higher value between the Account Balance or Account Equity.
  • Subtract 3% of that value from that value to set the Daily Drawdown Threshold for the next trading day.
Worked example
Account Balance$105,000
Account Equity$107,000 (Higher)
Threshold($107,000 × 3%) = $3,210
$107,000 - $3,210$103,790
Your balance/equity must stay above $103,790 for that day.

Optional 2% Daily Drawdown: At checkout, the Rules section has a Daily loss limit toggle. Choose 2% to lower the price of the challenge. It is available on every platform. If you choose it, the limit is 2% in both Step 1 and the funded stage, and it is calculated the same way. You can't change it after purchase.

Example: if the higher of your balance or equity at the 5:00 PM EST reset is $100,000, your threshold for the day is $100,000 - $2,000 = $98,000.

2. Maximum Loss Limit (Hard Breach) — 6% (Static)

This is your Absolute Floor. Your account equity or balance must never drop below 94% of your starting capital.

How is it calculated?

  • Maximum Loss = Initial Account Size − 6%

Worked example
Account Size$100,000
$100,000 X 6%$6,000
$100,000 - $6,000$94,000.
Your Absolute Floor: Your account equity/balance must never drop below $94,000.

Rewards & Profit Split

Once you are a Funded GOAT Trader, the following rules apply:

Profit Split: 80% (Traders keep 80% of their profit).

Payout Cycle: Bi-weekly (Every 14 days).

Daily Profit Cap: Funded accounts have a $3,000 daily profit limit.

*Note: Profits exceeding this are deducted but do NOT result in an account breach.

Initial 2 Payout Caps: For the first two payouts, withdrawals are capped at 6% of the initial account size or $10,000 (whichever is lower). This restriction is removed after the 2nd successful payout.
