When can I withdraw my affiliate commission?

Updated 14 November 2026

You can request affiliate commission 30 days after it's credited to your account.

The 30-day wait

Each commission has its own 30 days, counted from the day it's credited.

  • Credited on 13 November: you can request it from 13 December.
  • Credited on 20 November: you can request it from 20 December.

If a sale is refunded or charged back, its commission is removed, even after its 30 days have passed.

Minimum amount

The minimum payout request is $100. Only commission that has passed its 30 days counts toward it.

How to request

Request your payout from the Affiliate section of your dashboard.

You need to complete identity verification (KYC) before your first payout. Payouts are sent by crypto or Skrill and usually take 2 days to process.

For commission rates and sub-affiliate commission, see How does the compensation structure work in the Affiliate program?
