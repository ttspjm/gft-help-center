All about Rewards and Profit Splits

Updated 27 July 2026

REWARD

At Goat Funded Trader, we offer multiple reward structures tailored to different account models, ensuring traders have flexibility in withdrawing their profits. Below is a breakdown of how rewards work for each model:

1-Step Model

  • Bi-weekly rewards: Traders can withdraw their profits every 14 days after placing their first trade.

2-Step Model

Standard Model

  • Bi-weekly rewards: Traders can withdraw their profits every 14 days after placing their first trade.
  • Simply request a reward, and your earnings will be processed accordingly.

GOAT Model

  • Traders have the flexibility to choose between:
    • On-demand first reward (withdraw at any time once eligible).
  • After the first reward, subsequent rewards will be processed bi-weekly.

PRO Model

  • Bi-weekly rewards: Traders can withdraw their profits every 14 days after placing their first trade.

3-Step Model

  • Bi-weekly rewards: Traders can withdraw their profits every 14 days after placing their first trade.

Instant Funding Model

Standard Instant Funding

  • Triple payday rewards, allowing traders to withdraw profits every 10 days.

GOAT Instant Funding

  • Bi-weekly rewards: Traders can withdraw profits every 14 days after placing their first trade.

Goat Blitz Model

  • Bi-weekly rewards: Traders can withdraw profits every 14 days after placing their first trade.

General Reward Conditions

  • All rewards must be requested through the trader dashboard.
  • To access the first on-demand reward, traders must have a minimum of 3% profit on their account.
  • Traders must have no open trades or pending orders to receive a reward.
  • Minimum withdrawal amount: $100 for funded account rewards and affiliate withdrawals.
  • Withdrawals are processed through Rise, Crypto, Skrill and Bank transfer (Bank transfer available in Kenya, South Africa, Egypt, Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire, Tanzania, Cameroon and Ghana only).
  • Your first payout includes an identity verification (KYC) step and signing the Trader Agreement, both completed when you request the payout. See "How are withdrawal/reward requests processed?" for details.
  • Once a payout is requested, your selected payout method is locked and cannot be changed.

PROFIT SPLIT

We offer competitive profit splits across all account models. Traders can also opt for an upgrade to a 100% profit split during checkout. Below is a detailed breakdown of profit splits for each model:

1-Step Model

Traders receive 80% of their profits. Example: If a trader earns a profit of $5,000, they will receive $4,000 as their total reward.

Upon checkout, you have the option to upgrade with an add-on to:
  • 100% PROFIT SPLIT

2-Step Model

Standard Model

Traders receive 80% of their profits. Example: If a trader earns a profit of $5,000, they will receive $4,000 as their total reward.

Upon checkout, you have the option to upgrade with an add-on to:
  • 100% PROFIT SPLIT
  • ON-DEMAND REWARD

PRO Model

Traders receive 80% of their profits. Example: If a trader earns a profit of $5,000, they will receive $4,000 as their total reward.

Upon checkout, you have the option to upgrade with an add-on to:
  • 100% PROFIT SPLIT
  • ON-DEMAND REWARD

GOAT Model

Traders receive 80% of their profits. Example: If a trader earns a profit of $5,000, they will receive $4,000 as their total reward.

  • On-Demand Reward Option(first reward only):

If traders choose this option, they will receive a 40% profit split for that specific reward.

Upon checkout, you have the option to upgrade with an add-on to:
  • 100% PROFIT SPLIT
