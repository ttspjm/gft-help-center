BOGO40 Promotion

Updated 5 October 2026

What is the BOGO40 Promotion?

BOGO40 is a limited-time promotion that gives you:

  • 40% off your purchase, and
  • 1 free account of the same size and model as a bonus reward.

Note: The free account provided under this promotion is the same size and model as the account you purchased.

How does the BOGO40 Promotion work?

  1. Purchase an eligible account using the BOGO40 promotion.
  2. You receive 40% off at checkout.
  3. Your free account is not issued immediately - it becomes available after your first payout is successfully received.

How will I receive my free account?

The free account is issued only after your first payout has been successfully received.

Important: If you have not received your first payout yet, the free account cannot be issued.

How do I claim the free account?

Once your first payout has been received, you must contact our support team to claim the free BOGO account.

What type of free account will I receive?

Your free BOGO40 account is the same size and model as the account you purchased.
