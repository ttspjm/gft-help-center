How does the scaling plan work?

Updated 27 July 2026

At Goat Funded Trader, we believe in rewarding traders who demonstrate consistency, discipline, and profitability. Our structured scaling plan allows traders to grow their capital, increase their profit split, and unlock exclusive benefits as they progress through their trading journey.

Level 1

Where your journey begins

Requirements:

2 months in the live funded phase

4 successful payouts

Rewards & Benefits:

Capital Boost: 15% increase

Profit Split: 90%

Daily Drawdown Limit: Increased by 1%

Maximum Drawdown Limit: Increased by 1%

Level 2

Building on experience

Requirements:

4 months in the live funded phase

6 successful payouts

Rewards & Benefits:

Capital Boost: 30% increase

Profit Split: 90%

Daily Drawdown Limit: Increased by 2%

Maximum Drawdown Limit: Increased by 2%

Weekly Payouts

Level 3

Proven consistency & discipline

Requirements:

5 months in the live funded phase

10 successful payouts

Rewards & Benefits:

Capital Boost: 40% increase

Profit Split: 92%

Daily Drawdown Limit: Increased by 3%

Maximum Drawdown Limit: Increased by 3%

Weekly Payouts

Free Challenge if you fail the account

Monthly Salary:

  • $300 for accounts 5K – 50K
  • $500 for accounts 100K – 200K

Level 4 – GOAT Trader

The ultimate milestone

Requirements:

6 months in the live funded phase

15 successful payouts

Rewards & Benefits:

Capital Boost: 50% increase

Profit Split: 95%

Daily Drawdown Limit: Increased by 4%

Maximum Drawdown Limit: Increased by 4%

Free Challenge if you fail the account

Monthly Salary:

  • $300 for accounts 5K – 50K
  • $500 for accounts 100K – 200K

Important Notes:

All scale-ups are calculated based on the original account size. If your account is merged, scaling will still be based on the initial account, not the merged size.

This structured plan ensures traders are rewarded for their consistency, allowing them to grow their capital and access premium benefits at each level.
