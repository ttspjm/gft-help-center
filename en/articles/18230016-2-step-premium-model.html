2-Step Premium Model

Updated 4 November 2026

2-Step Premium is a two-step challenge with an 85% profit split. You pass two evaluation steps, then trade a simulated funded account and request payouts under the rules below. It is a different model from Instant Premium, which is an Instant Funding model.

Account sizes: $5,000, $10,000, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000 and $200,000.

Options you choose at checkout

  • Step 2 profit target: 5% or 6%.
  • Daily drawdown: 4% or 3%. Your choice applies in every step, including the funded account.

Step 1 (Evaluation)

To pass Step 1, you must:

  • Achieve a 10% profit target.
  • Complete at least 3 profitable days.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits.

After passing Step 1, you automatically proceed to Step 2.

Step 2 (Evaluation)

To pass Step 2, you must:

  • Achieve the profit target you chose at checkout, 5% or 6%.
  • Complete at least 3 profitable days.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits.

After passing Step 2, your entire evaluation is reviewed. If successful, you receive your funded account.

Step 3 (Funded)

  • Complete at least 7 profitable days, each with a minimum of 0.5% profit, to be eligible for a payout. The count resets after each payout.
  • Follow all trading rules and loss limits.

What is a minimum profitable day?

A minimum profitable day is a day on which you close at least one position and your profit for that day is at least 0.5% of the initial account balance, net of fees and commissions.

Account violations (hard breach)

Breaking either of these limits closes the account immediately.

1. Daily Drawdown Limit, 3% or 4%

At 5:00 PM EST, we check the higher value between your account balance and account equity. We subtract your daily drawdown percentage from that value to set your limit for the next trading day.

Example

  • Account balance: $105,000
  • Account equity: $107,000 (the higher value)
  • At 4%: $107,000 x 4% = $4,280, so your balance and equity must stay above $102,720 for that day.
  • At 3%: $107,000 x 3% = $3,210, so your balance and equity must stay above $103,790 for that day.

2. Maximum Loss Limit, 10% (Static)

Your account balance or equity must never drop below 90% of your initial account size. The limit does not move as your balance grows.

Example

  • Account size: $100,000
  • $100,000 x 10% = $10,000
  • Your balance and equity must never drop below $90,000.

Rewards and profit split

  • Profit split: 85%. You keep 85% of your profit.
  • Payouts: anytime, once you have completed 7 minimum profitable days, each with at least 0.5% profit. The 7 days reset after each payout, so you need 7 more before your next request.
  • Daily profit cap: funded accounts have a $3,000 daily profit limit. Profit above this is deducted, but it is not a breach.
  • First two payouts: a cap applies. See Funded Account Reward Guidelines.
  • Best trade vs worst trade: your largest winning trade must be at least as big as your largest losing trade, so a tie passes. See How payouts work.

Optional add-on at checkout

  • 100% Profit Split

Other rules

All other trading and risk management rules apply, including the margin rule in "All or Nothing" trading strategies. For leverage, see What is the account leverage?. For how to request a payout, see How payouts work.
