IP Address and Account Access Policy

Updated 6 October 2026

To maintain a fair trading environment and protect the integrity of our evaluation and funded programs, Goat Funded Trader actively monitors account access and IP address activity across all stages.

Account Ownership

All trading accounts must be operated exclusively by the account owner.

Sharing account credentials, allowing third parties to access your account, or using any account management or "pass your challenge" services is strictly prohibited and may result in account breaches and permanent restrictions from our services.

Evaluation and Funded Accounts

The geographical region of the IP address used to purchase a challenge, as well as the region used to access and trade a funded account, should remain reasonably consistent over time.

Significant or unexplained discrepancies between purchase location, trading location, or repeated regional IP changes may trigger a review by our Risk Team.

Travel and Change of Location

We understand that traders may travel or temporarily change location.

If you plan to access or trade your account from a different country or region, we strongly recommend informing our support team in advance to avoid unnecessary account reviews or interruptions.

If unusual IP activity is detected, our Risk Team may contact you and request additional verification, including but not limited to:

  • Travel tickets or booking confirmations;
  • Passport stamps or other travel documentation;
  • Video verification or other reasonable proof of location.

Failure to provide sufficient evidence that the account has been operated solely by the account owner may result in account restrictions, account breaches, or permanent suspension from our services.

VPNs, VPS, and Remote Access Tools

From October 6, 2026, the use of any hosting or IP-masking or routing service during trading activity is strictly prohibited on all accounts. This includes VPNs, VPSs, proxies, remote or hosted environments, and any other hosting or routing solution used to access or trade on the trading platform.

If such a service is detected during trading activity, the account is subject to a straight breach, and any payout generated through that trading activity is not eligible for payment.

The only exception is the first login: if you need a VPN to locate or connect to our trading server for the first time, you may use it for that connection only, and it must be disconnected before any trading takes place.

Full details: Can I use a VPN, VPS, proxy, or any other hosting service while trading?

Important Notice

Repeated unexplained IP changes, simultaneous access from multiple locations, shared devices, or any activity suggesting that an account is not being operated independently may lead to a full investigation by our Risk Team.

Any violation of this policy may result in account breaches, payout rejection, suspension of services, or permanent exclusion from Goat Funded Trader.
