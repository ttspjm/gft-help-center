How to Become an Affiliate?

Updated 21 April 2025

To become an affiliate, simply create an account on our dashboard. Once registered, navigate to the "Affiliate" section and complete the registration by filling out all the required details. Our team will review your application, and once approved, you'll gain access to the tools needed to start earning rewards by referring clients to us.
