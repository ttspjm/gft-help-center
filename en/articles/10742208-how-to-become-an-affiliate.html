How to Become an Affiliate?

Updated 13 November 2026

You apply to the affiliate program from your Goat Funded Trader dashboard.

How to apply

  1. Log in to your Goat Funded Trader dashboard. If you don't have an account yet, create one first.
  2. Open the Affiliate section.
  3. Fill in the application form with all the required details.

Our team reviews every application. Once you're approved, you'll find your affiliate link and your invite link in the Affiliate section.

If another affiliate invited you

Apply through their invite link. That makes you their sub-affiliate, and it doesn't reduce your own commission.

If you already joined without their link, you'd need to apply again through it to be linked to them.

What you earn

12% on your sales until you've referred 100 different customers, 15% from your 100th customer onward, and 5% of the sales made by any affiliates you invite. See How does the compensation structure work in the Affiliate program? for the details and an example.
