GFT Mobile App

Updated 27 July 2026

Can I log in with my existing account?

Yes. You can log in using the same credentials you use on the GFT dashboard. No new account setup is required.

Are there rewards for downloading or rating the app?

Yes! We’ve added two new reward tasks under the Rewards section:

  • Download the GFT App → 40 Points
  • Rate the GFT App on the iOS App Store or Google Play Store → 60 Points

Why haven’t my reward points been added yet?

Both of these app-related tasks require manual verification.

  • Points are not added instantly
  • Verification may take some time depending on request volume

Please be patient while our team reviews and approves submissions.

What proof is required for reward verification?

You may be asked to provide:

  • A screenshot showing the app installed on your device
  • A screenshot of your app rating/review on the App Store or Google Play Store

Make sure screenshots are clear and show the correct app.
