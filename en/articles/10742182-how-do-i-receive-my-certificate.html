How do I receive my Certificate?

Updated 27 July 2026

You will receive your Funded Certificate after successfully passing the Evaluation Phase and obtaining your Simulated Funded Account.

Additionally, a Reward Certificate will be issued immediately after your reward has been processed. Both type of certificates will be available for download directly from your dashboard and we’ll also be sent to you via email as soon as they are created.
