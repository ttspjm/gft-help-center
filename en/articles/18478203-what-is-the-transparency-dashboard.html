What is the transparency dashboard?

Updated 17 November 2026

The transparency dashboard at goatfundedtrader.com/transparency is a public page with the numbers behind Goat Funded Trader. Anyone can open it, with or without an account. It shows:

  • Traders and accounts: registered traders, new sign-ups, active challenge and funded accounts, and the capital funded accounts trade
  • Rewards: the total paid to traders, how many traders have been paid, the biggest rewards, the latest rewards as they go out, and how fast they reach traders
  • Trading results: net P&L, average profit and loss, and the most traded instruments
  • How long challenge accounts stay active before they pass, fail or expire
  • How our rewards and website traffic compare with other prop firms, from third-party sources

Some detailed views, like rewards by country and account-size breakdowns, open after you sign up. Rewards paid before our current tracking system started aren't in the all-time total. The page shows that amount separately.
