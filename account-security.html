IP, devices and accounts

IP Address and Account Access Policy

To maintain a fair trading environment and protect the integrity of our evaluation and funded programs, Goat Funded Trader actively monitors account access and IP address activity across all stages.

Account Ownership

All trading accounts must be operated exclusively by the account owner.

Sharing account credentials, allowing third parties to access your account, or using any account management or "pass your challenge" services is strictly prohibited and may result in account breaches and permanent restrictions from our services.

Evaluation and Funded Accounts

The geographical region of the IP address used to purchase a challenge, as well as the region used to access and trade a funded account, should remain reasonably consistent over time.

Significant or unexplained discrepancies between purchase location, trading location, or repeated regional IP changes may trigger a review by our Risk Team.

Travel and Change of Location

We understand that traders may travel or temporarily change location.

If you plan to access or trade your account from a different country or region, we strongly recommend informing our support team in advance to avoid unnecessary account reviews or interruptions.

If unusual IP activity is detected, our Risk Team may contact you and request additional verification, including but not limited to:

  • Travel tickets or booking confirmations;
  • Passport stamps or other travel documentation;
  • Video verification or other reasonable proof of location.

Failure to provide sufficient evidence that the account has been operated solely by the account owner may result in account restrictions, account breaches, or permanent suspension from our services.

VPNs, VPS, and Remote Access Tools

VPN Usage
Yes, the use of a VPN is allowed. However, if our Risk Team detects unusual or inconsistent geographical login patterns, we reserve the right to request proof of the VPN service used to verify the legitimacy of the activity.

VPS Usage
For all accounts purchased from August 12, 2026 onwards, the use of a VPS (Virtual Private Server) is strictly prohibited on the following account types:

  • One Step
  • Two Step
  • Instant Premium
  • Pay Later

The use of a VPS remains permitted on:

  • Instant GOAT
  • Instant Hero

Accounts purchased before August 12, 2026 are not affected by this new VPS restriction.

Important Notice

Repeated unexplained IP changes, simultaneous access from multiple locations, shared devices, or any activity suggesting that an account is not being operated independently may lead to a full investigation by our Risk Team.

Any violation of this policy may result in account breaches, payout rejection, suspension of services, or permanent exclusion from Goat Funded Trader.

Shared Device Usage

Traders are strictly prohibited from accessing or operating their account on any shared mobile or desktop device. Detection of account activity from a shared device, especially one linked to multiple user accounts will result in immediate disqualification from payout eligibility and may lead to account termination.

The same applies to payout details. A wallet address or payment account that appears on more than one GFT account is treated as a breach at the point of the payout request, with no review. See How payouts work: eligibility, request, processing and arrival

Can I create multiple accounts on GFT?

No. Each person is only permitted to register once on our platform. Creating multiple accounts to exploit our offers, promotions or benefits is strictly prohibited. Any accounts found in violation of this policy will be permanently banned and immediately cancelled without notice.

We encourage all our traders to engage with our platform fairly and in accordance with our terms of service.

Can I Have Multiple Registered Accounts?

No, each user is allowed to have only one registered account with Goat Funded Trader.

However, with that single registered account, you may purchase multiple challenges.

Can I merge accounts?

Merging combines your simulated Funded Accounts into a single account with a larger balance, so you can trade with more capital under one account. Here's everything you need to know about when it's possible.

Which accounts can be merged?

Only simulated Funded Accounts that came from an Evaluation model can be merged.

These can never be merged:

  • Evaluation accounts, at any stage.
  • Instant Funding accounts of any kind, including Instant GOAT, Instant PRO, Instant HERO, Instant Premium and Instant Standard. An Instant account cannot be merged with another Instant account, and it cannot be merged with an account from any other model.

Conditions for merging

You can merge your eligible Funded Accounts when all of the following are true across every account you want to merge:

  • No trades placed - each account must still be at its initial balance. This means the account is either brand new (untraded) or has just been reset following a payout.
  • Same account type - all accounts must be the same type.
  • Same profit split - all accounts must share the same profit split.
  • Same add-ons - all accounts must have the same add-ons, or none of them have any add-ons.

Do the account sizes have to match?

No. Account sizes do not need to be the same. For example, a $5,000 account and a $50,000 account can be merged into a single $55,000 account, as long as all the conditions above are met.

Is there a limit?

The only cap is a combined total of $400,000, our permitted maximum allocation per trader. You can merge as many accounts as you like, as long as their balances add up to no more than that.

How do I request a merge?

If your accounts meet all the conditions above, reach out to our support team and we'll help you complete the merge.
