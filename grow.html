How do Resets work?

Goat Funded Trader's Reset feature gives you a second chance after breaching your account. Instead of purchasing a brand-new Challenge and starting your journey from the beginning, you can purchase a Reset and continue from the phase where the breach occurred.

This means that if you've already passed one or more phases, your progress is preserved, you only need to repeat the phase you breached.

Note: Resets are only available for accounts purchases made from June 15th, 2026 onward - accounts purchased before that date are not eligible for a reset. Additionally, Account Resets are not available for Pay Later models or Goat Stride models.

Accounts received through GOAT Points, giveaways or competitions do not have the reset option, so if your account came from one of those, the option not appearing is expected and nothing is wrong.

What happens when I purchase a Reset?

Once your Reset purchase is completed, a brand-new account will be issued. Your previous account is not restored or modified and cannot be used anymore.

Your new account will:

  • Start with the same initial balance as your original purchase.
  • Have the same Challenge type and account size as originally purchased.
  • Begin directly from the phase you're resetting.

Example:

If you purchased a $100,000 Two-Step Challenge, passed Phase 1, and then breached Phase 2, your Reset will issue a new $100,000 Phase 2 account. You will not need to complete Phase 1 again.

Which phase will I restart from?

You always restart from the phase where the breach occurred.

For example:

  • Breach Phase 1 → Restart from Phase 1.
  • Breach Phase 2 after passing Phase 1 → Restart from Phase 2.
  • Breach a Funded Account → Receive a brand-new Funded Account without repeating the evaluation process.

When can I purchase a Reset?

You can purchase a Reset at any time.

This means you don't have to wait until your account is breached. If your account is still active but you're in a significant drawdown and prefer to start that phase over, you can choose to purchase a Reset instead.

If your account has already been breached, you have 14 calendar days from the date of the breach to purchase a Reset.

After the 14-day period expires, the Reset option for that account will no longer be available, and you'll need to purchase a new Challenge if you wish to continue.

Important: Once an account has been breached, the Reset must be purchased within 14 calendar days. After this period, the Reset option permanently expires for that account.

Why is Phase 1 cheaper?

If you breach before passing any phase, you'll receive a 7% discount compared to purchasing the same Challenge again.

This gives traders a more aﬀordable way to try again.

Why do later phases cost more?

The later you are in your journey, the more valuable your Reset becomes.

Rather than forcing you to restart from the beginning, a Reset preserves the progress you've already earned and allows you to continue directly from the phase where you breached your account.

Can I reset a Funded Account?

Yes.

If you breach your Funded Account, you can purchase a Funded Reset and receive a brand-new Funded Account without having to complete the evaluation process again.

How many Resets can I purchase?

Each phase can only be reset once.

For example, on a Two-Step Challenge you may purchase:

  • One Phase 1 Reset.
  • One Phase 2 Reset.
  • One Funded Reset.

If you breach the same phase again after using its Reset, you'll need to purchase a new Challenge.

Why should I purchase a Reset instead of a new Challenge?

A Reset allows you to continue from the phase where you breached your account instead of starting your evaluation from scratch.

This preserves the progress you've already earned, gets you back to trading faster, and eliminates the need to repeat phases you've already successfully completed.

Can I get a discount on a Reset?

Yes! From time to time, Goat Funded Trader may oﬀer special promotions or discounts on Reset purchases.

Be sure to regularly check the My offers section of your dashboard, where any available Reset promotions will be displayed.

We may also announce limited-time Reset promotions on our oﬃcial social media channels, so make sure to follow us to stay up to date and avoid missing any missing offers.

GOAT Points

The GOAT Points Program is designed to reward you for every action you take as a trader.
You can now earn points automatically from your purchases and activities — and redeem them for exclusive GFT benefits inside your Trader Dashboard.

How It Works

  • Earn automatically: For every $1 spent, you earn 1 GOAT Point instantly.
  • Complete tasks: Gain even more points by finishing trading challenges and activities.
  • Redeem rewards: Use your points to unlock valuable trading perks.
  • Repeat: Keep trading, keep earning, and grow your GOAT status.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What are GOAT Points?

GOAT Points are GFT’s loyalty points that reward you for both trading and purchases.
You can use them to unlock powerful benefits and exclusive rewards in your account.

2. How do I earn GOAT Points?

You can earn points in two main ways:

  • Purchases: Earn 1 point for every $1 spent on any GFT product or service.
  • Trading Tasks: Complete certain tasks to gain additional points.

3. When are my points credited?

Your GOAT Points are credited instantly after:

  • A qualifying purchase, or
  • The completion of a task.

4. Where can I check my GOAT Points balance?

All your earned points, available tasks, and redeemable rewards are visible in your Trader Dashboard → GOAT Points section.

5. What can I spend my GOAT Points on?

Redeem your points for exclusive rewards such as:

  • Free Instant or Challenge Accounts
  • 1:50 Extra Leverage
  • +1% Extra Drawdown (DD)
  • +10% Account Size Increase
  • …and many more powerful trading benefits!

6. How do I redeem my rewards?

Simply:

  1. Go to your Trader Dashboard → GOAT Points
  2. Choose the reward you want
  3. Click Redeem
    Your GOAT Points will be deducted automatically.

7. Is there a limit to how many points I can earn?

No — there’s no cap!
The more you trade, purchase, and complete tasks, the more GOAT Points you’ll collect.

8. Who can earn GOAT Points?

All GFT traders are automatically enrolled in the GOAT Points Program. No extra sign-up required.

9. Why should I use GOAT Points?

Because consistency now comes with real benefits.
Every trade and every purchase earns you something extra — turning your activity into rewards that make trading even more rewarding.

10. How do I get started?

  1. Log in to your Trader Dashboard
  2. Open the GOAT Points section
  3. Start completing tasks and earning points
  4. Redeem your points for exclusive GFT rewards

Tip: Keep checking your dashboard — new tasks and rewards are added regularly!
