Is hedging allowed?

No, hedging is strictly prohibited. This includes:

  • Hedging within the same trading account.
  • Hedging between multiple accounts owned by the same trader.
  • Hedging between accounts owned by different traders.
  • Hedging between a GoatFundedTrader (GFT) account another Firm’s account, either if the two accounts are owned by the same person or by two different people.

Hedging is defined as opening two opposite positions on the same asset, regardless of lot size or calculated risk. This rule is in place to maintain fair and transparent trading practices and to protect the integrity of our trading environment.

If we detect any hedging activity—especially between a GFT account and an external firm—the account will be breached immediately, and the user will be permanently banned from our platform. In the case of a Simulated Funded Account, the trader will not be eligible for any rewards.

Can I use Expert Advisors (EAs)?

You can use an Expert Advisor only if you developed it yourself. Third-party EAs are not allowed at any time. This includes purchased, shared and off-the-shelf EAs, and any EA marketed to pass evaluations. Our team has the right to request proof of ownership at any time, for example the source code. If you cannot provide it, the account is treated as in breach.

High-frequency trading (HFT) systems and Gold Arbitrage EAs are not allowed in any case, and your EA must still follow the prohibited trading practices rule.

Note: If your EA requires a VPS to run, keep in mind that VPS usage is restricted depending on your account type. For accounts purchased from August 12, 2026 onwards:

  • VPS is prohibited on:
    • One Step
    • Two Step
    • Instant Premium
    • Pay Later
  • VPS remains permitted on:
    • Instant GOAT
    • Instant Hero

Accounts purchased before August 12, 2026 are not affected by this restriction. If your account type falls under the prohibited list and your EA depends on a VPS to function, you will not be able to run that EA on this account.

Using Signals

Signal services and copying trades from other people are not permitted on GOAT Funded Trader accounts.

Copying trades from signal providers, trading groups, third-party services, or other traders is considered a prohibited trading practice and may result in account termination.

Copy trading between your own accounts is allowed. You can freely copy trades between your own Goat Funded Trader accounts. This includes copying from one evaluation account to another, and between evaluation and funded accounts in either direction. This applies to all accounts, whenever they were purchased.

All trading decisions must be made independently by the account holder. Traders are expected to manage their own positions and execute trades based on their own analysis and judgment.

If you are unsure whether a particular service or trading method is permitted, please contact our Support team before using it.

Does Goat Funded Trader Allow "All or Nothing" Trading Strategies?

At Goat Funded Trader, we have a strict policy against high-risk, "all or nothing" trading strategies. Our goal is to support traders who demonstrate strong risk management, consistent performance, and the ability to generate sustainable profits. These are the traders we partner with, offering simulated funded accounts of up to $400,000.

We define gambling-style trading as any trade where more than 80% of available margin is used in a trading idea. Such excessive risk exposure indicates a lack of proper risk management, which goes against the principles we uphold.

Trading strategies that rely on high-risk, all-or-nothing approaches are not permitted. If a trader engages in such activities, it may lead to profit deductions or phase resets.

At Goat Funded Trader, we prioritize disciplined and strategic trading to ensure long-term success in the financial markets.

What is the minimum holding time for trades?

For funded accounts, any profit from a trade that is open for less than the minimum holding time is considered invalid and is removed when a payout is requested.

The minimum holding time depends on when the account was purchased:

  • Purchased before 6 October 2026 (00:00 UTC): 2 minutes (120 seconds)
  • Purchased from 6 October 2026 (00:00 UTC) onward: 1 minute (60 seconds)

A funded account follows the purchase date of the challenge it came from. If you reset a challenge that was purchased before 6 October 2026, it stays at 2 minutes, even if the reset happens after that date.

Any losses from trades closed before the minimum holding time remain and are the trader's responsibility.

This means you may open and close positions faster than the minimum holding time if you wish. Profits from those trades are not included in payout calculations, while losses still count.

Having profits removed for this reason is not a breach or violation of funded account rules. It only affects the payout amount.

This rule applies only to funded accounts and does not affect evaluation (challenge) accounts.

This policy helps ensure fair trading conditions and aligns with the liquidity and risk management standards required for consistent and sustainable trading across all funded traders.

Is News Trading allowed?

1. Can I trade during news events with Goat Funded Traders?

Yes. Trading during news events is allowed and does not violate any Goat Funded Traders rules.

2. Are there any limitations on profits generated during high-impact news events?

Yes. Any trade that is opened or closed within 5 minutes before or after a high-impact news release (marked with a red folder on ForexFactory.com or Myfxbook.com) can generate a maximum profit of 1% of the account’s initial balance per trade.

This includes trades closed manually or automatically through:

  • Stop Loss
  • Take Profit
  • Pending Orders

3. Does this policy apply to both the challenge and funded phases?

Yes, this rule applies equally to both the challenge phase and the funded phase of your account.

4. What happens if profits exceed the 1% limit during these times?

The limit applies to each trade separately. If the profit from a single trade in the restricted time exceeds 1% of the initial account balance, the excess profit on that trade will be removed.

This adjustment will not result in any kind of breach or penalty to the account. This deduction typically happens during account reviews.

Do you allow trading the weekend gap?

At Goat Funded Trader, we fully allow traders to hold trades over the weekend. However, we do not allow opening trades solely to take advantage of the price gap that occurs when the market reopens on Monday.

Any trades opened during the last three (3) market hours on Friday and closed within the first three (3) market hours on Monday will be subject to review. For funded accounts only, any profits generated from these trades will be removed.

This does not constitute a violation of any kind and will not result in penalties or account termination. It is strictly a profit adjustment.

We encourage traders to focus on consistent, strategy-based performance rather than attempting to exploit weekend market gaps.

What Time Does A Trading Day Start?

Trading days are calculated based on 5 PM EST.

A new trading day starts at 5:00:01 PM EST and runs until 4:59:59 PM EST the next day.

A trade counts toward the trading day in which it is closed. A trade closed after 5 PM EST counts toward the next day's trading day.

For example:

  • A trade closed on Monday at 4 PM EST counts toward Monday's trading day.
  • A trade closed on Monday at 6 PM EST counts toward Tuesday's trading day.

This timing aligns with the global forex market session rollover, ensuring consistency across all trader accounts.

What is considered a Trading Day?

A trading day runs from 5 PM EST to 5 PM EST.

A day is counted as a trading day when you close at least one position during it and the minimum daily profit requirement for your model and phase is met. Opening a trade without closing it does not count.

Where a profit requirement applies, the day must close with a profit of at least 0.5% of the initial account balance, net of fees and commissions. These days are called minimum profitable days.

The number of days required for each model is listed in What are the Minimum Profitable Days?

What Is the Consistency Rule?

Why Do We Have a Consistency Rule?

The Consistency Rule is designed to encourage steady and sustainable trading performance. It helps ensure that traders are not relying on one unusually large trading day.

What Is the Consistency Rule?

The Consistency Rule means that no single trading day can account for X% or more of your total profits during the payout period.

The consistency percentage may vary depending on the challenge or funding model.

You can check whether your challenge has a Consistency Rule in our detailed model FAQs: https://help.goatfundedtrader.com/en/collections/11811982-models

Example

For example, if the Consistency Rule is 15% and your highest profit day should not exceed 15% of the total profit earned.

If your total profit earned is $5000, your highest trading day should not exceed (5000 X 15% ) = $750.

What Happens If I Do Not Meet the Consistency Rule?

Not meeting the Consistency Rule will not result in account termination or an account breach. However, you will not be able to request a payout until your highest profit day is below X% of your total profits for the payout period.

What Should I Do Next?

You should continue trading and generating profits until your highest profit day represents less than X% of your total profits.

Keep in mind that in order to decrease the consistency %, your max profit should be less than your current highest trading day.

How Are Partial Trades Counted for the Consistency Rule?

Each partial close counts on the trading day it happens. If you close one position in several parts on different days, the profit or loss from each part goes to the day that part was closed, and the consistency check uses those daily figures. No single day carries the whole trade.

Example of Partial Trade Calculation

You open one large trade and close a third on Monday, a third on Tuesday and the rest on Wednesday. Monday's part counts toward Monday's profit, Tuesday's toward Tuesday, Wednesday's toward Wednesday.

Trading days run 5 PM to 5 PM EST, so a partial close at 6 PM EST on Monday counts toward Tuesday's trading day.

Is there any inactivity limit?

Understanding GoatFundedTrader's Inactivity Policy

If no trades are placed on your account for 30 consecutive days, it will be breached due to inactivity. This rule applies to both Evaluation and Simulated Funded Accounts. Once breached, the account cannot be reinstated, and the fee will not be refunded. Make sure to stay active to keep your account in good standing.

To ensure the account remains active, consider executing at least one trade every 29 days to provide a buffer.

Common Questions About Inactivity

  1. Is there a minimum number of trading days requirement each month?
    No, the inactivity policy is solely based on ensuring at least one trade within a 30-day period. The monthly minimum trading day requirements apply only when progressing accounts or requesting payouts.
