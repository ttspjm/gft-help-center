1. Max Total Drawdown – 4% (Trailing)

What does this mean?

  • The maximum total drawdown of Instant Pro accounts is a trailing drawdown set at 4% of the equity value. This means that your maximum loss limit adjusts based on your account’s performance. If your equity increases, your maximum drawdown limit also moves up. However, it does not decrease if your equity drops, ensuring a dynamic but protective risk management system.
Worked example
For a $100,000 account:
If your account reaches $104,000, your new trailing drawdown limit becomes $99,840 (4% below your account’s equity)
If your account then drops to $103,000, your new trailing drawdown limit will still be $99,840.
Your drawdown limit follows your Equity value and remains 4% of that value.

N.B - The Maximum Loss Limit resets after each payout.

Understanding the Maximum Loss Per Trade Rule

If your floating profit and loss (PnL) drops below -2% of your account balance at any moment, the account will be permanently closed. This rule enforces strict risk management and prevents excessive losses.

For example: For a $50,000 account, if your combined open trade losses reach -$1,000 (-2%), the account will be immediately closed.

Minimum profitable day requirement for Instant Pro accounts

Traders on Instant Pro accounts must complete at least 5 profitable days before requesting a reward. These days do not have to be consecutive. To count as a minimum profitable day, the day must close with a profit of at least 0.5% of the initial balance, net of fees and commissions.

Consistency Rule

What is the consistency rule?

  • The consistency rule ensures steady and sustainable profits over time. It states that no single trading day can account for more than 20% of your total profits during a payout period.
Worked example
If you make $2,000 in one trading day, your total profits must reach at least $10,000 to be eligible to request a payout. You cannot request a payout until the highest profit day is no more than 20% of the total profits for the period.

What Happens if I Violate the 20% Rule?

Will my account be terminated?

  • No, violating the 20% rule will not result in account termination. However, you will be unable to request a payout until your highest profit trading day is no more than 20% of your total profits.

What should I do next?

  • Continue trading and generating profits until your highest profit day is at or below the 20% threshold. Once this happens, you will be eligible to request a payout again.
