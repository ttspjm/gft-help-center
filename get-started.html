Get started

When will I receive my credentials?

When you purchase, your credentials arrive by email immediately and also appear in your dashboard.

When you pass a phase, your next account is created automatically. You do not need to request it. Passing an intermediate phase (for example Phase 1 on a 2-step model) issues your next credentials immediately. After your final evaluation phase, your account goes through a review that typically takes up to 2 business days; you receive a confirmation email while it runs, then your funded account credentials arrive by email and appear in your dashboard.

You do not need to complete identity verification (KYC) or sign the Trader Agreement to receive credentials. Both happen later, when you request your first payout.

What happens when you pass an evaluation phase

  1. Your account stops trading at the moment you hit the target.
  2. Your evaluation is reviewed, typically up to 2 business days. You receive a confirmation email during this time.
  3. Your next account is created.
  4. Credentials are emailed to the address on your account, and appear in your dashboard.

On multi-phase models you move to the next phase automatically. On 2 Step and 3 Step models, passing an intermediate phase moves you straight on with no review.

Only the final evaluation phase triggers the full review before funding.

Pay Later is different

On the Pay Later model you pay $5 up front and the balance only after passing. Your funded account is not issued until the remaining fee is paid.

  • You have 30 days from receiving the activation link to pay.
  • If you do not pay within 30 days the account expires, and the deadline cannot be extended and a new link cannot be issued for the same account.
  • Profits made during evaluation cannot be used to pay the fee.

Where to find your credentials

  1. Your dashboard.Log in and open the account. This is the fastest route and does not depend on email delivery.
  2. Your email, sent to the address registered on your account.

If the email has not arrived, check spam first, then check the dashboard. The dashboard is authoritative. If credentials are visible there, the account exists and is ready, regardless of what your inbox shows.

Common questions

I passed but I have not received anything. Check your dashboard first. If the account is there, you can start trading immediately using those credentials. If it is not there after 2 business days, contact support with your account number.

Do I need to request my next phase account? No. It is created automatically.

My credentials do not work. Confirm you are using the right platform (MT5, Match-Trader, and so on) and the right server for that account. Each account has its own server, and using a previous account's server is the most common cause.

Can I keep trading my evaluation account after passing? No. It stops at the moment you pass.

What Is A Minimum Profitable Day?

  • A minimum profitable day is a day on which you close at least one position and your profit for that day is at least 0.5% of the initial account balance ($500), net of fees and commissions.
  • A trading day runs from 5:00 PM EST to 5:00 PM EST. A position closed after 5:00 PM EST counts toward the next day (What Time Does A Trading Day Start?).

Account Violations (Hard Breach)

Breaking either of these limits closes the account immediately.

1. Daily Drawdown Limit 3%

How is it calculated?

  • At 5:00 PM EST, we check the higher value of your Account Balance and Account Equity.
  • We subtract 3% of that value to set your Daily Drawdown Threshold for the next trading day.
Worked example
Account Balance$105,000
Account Equity$107,000 (Higher)
Threshold$107,000 × 3% = $3,210
$107,000 - $3,210$103,790
Your balance/equity must stay above $103,790 for that day.

2. Maximum Loss Limit 8% (Static)

This is your absolute floor. Your account equity or balance must never drop below 92% of your initial account balance. The floor is fixed and does not move up as your profit grows.

How is it calculated?

  • Maximum Loss = Initial Account Size - 8%
Worked example
Account Size$100,000
$100,000 × 8%$8,000
$100,000 - $8,000$92,000
Your Absolute Floor: Your account equity/balance must never drop below $92,000.

Floating Loss Limit 2% (Funded Only)

In the Funded stage, the combined floating (unrealized) loss of all your open positions must not reach 2% of your initial account balance.

The limit is a fixed amount for the life of the account: $2,000. It stays $2,000 even if your balance grows.

What happens if I reach it?

The first time your open positions reach the limit, you receive an email. Your positions stay open, the account stays active and you can keep trading, but your profit split is reduced to 50%.

The second time, the account is breached and permanently closed.

When does it count as a second time?

When the limit is reached, we record the group of positions open at that moment. While that same group stays open, its loss moving around or below the limit does not count again.

If you close one of those positions or open a new one, the group changes. If the new group reaches the 2% limit, that is the second time and the account is closed.

Worked example
The limit is $2,000.
Positions A and B reach a combined floating loss of $2,000. This is the first time: your profit split is reduced to 50% and you keep trading.
A and B stay open and their loss moves around $2,000. This still counts as the first time.
You open Position C, and A, B and C together reach a $2,000 floating loss. This is the second time, and the account is closed.

Consistency Rule 40% (Funded Only)

What is the consistency rule?

  • No single trading day can account for more than 40% of your total profit in a payout period. A best day of exactly 40% is eligible.
  • The rule does not apply to Step 1 or Step 2.
Worked example
If your best trading day made $800, your total profit must reach at least $2,000 before you can request a payout.

What happens if I go over 40%?

  • Your account is not closed. You can't request a payout until your best day is back at 40% or less of your total profit.
  • Keep trading. As your total profit grows, your best day becomes a smaller share of it.

Best Trade vs Worst Trade (Funded Only)

Before you can request a payout, your largest winning trade must be at least as big as your largest losing trade.

Every closed trade counts on its own. If you close a position in parts, each part is a separate trade. Each trade's result includes commission and swap.

Until your first payout, the check covers every trade on your funded account. Each payout resets it, and from then on it only looks at trades closed after your last payout.

Worked example
If your largest loss this payout period is $400, your largest win needs to be $400 or more.

This is not a breach and does not affect your account. If you haven't met it yet, keep trading until you do.

Rewards & Profit Split

Once you are a Funded GOAT Trader, the following rules apply:

Profit Split: 80% (you keep 80% of your profit).

Daily Profit Cap: A funded account can make a maximum of $3,000 profit per trading day. Profit above $3,000 is deducted from the account. This is not a breach.

Payout Cap: You can withdraw a maximum of $2,000 per payout. Any profit above this is deducted from the account balance. The cap applies to each payout, not to the total you can withdraw from the account.

What happens if I fail my challenge or funded account?

If you fail your challenge or funded account at any stage, that account will be closed and can no longer be used. However, you don't need to start over from scratch — you can purchase a Reset to continue from the phase where the breach occurred, preserving any phases you've already passed.

If you breach Phase 1 → your Reset restarts from Phase 1.

If you breach Phase 2 (after passing Phase 1) → your Reset restarts from Phase 2.

If you breach a Funded Account → your Reset issues a brand-new Funded Account, without repeating the evaluation. This is only possible if the funded account has not received a payout. Once it has, it cannot be reset and you'll need to purchase a new account to continue.

Resets must be purchased within 14 calendar days of the breach, and each phase can only be reset once. If you don't purchase a Reset within that window, or if you breach the same phase again after using its Reset, you'll need to purchase a new Challenge to continue.

Note: Resets are only available for accounts purchased from June 15th, 2026 onward — accounts purchased before that date are not eligible for a reset and will need to start a new Challenge if breached.

For full details on pricing, timing, and how many Resets you can use, see the How do Resets work? FAQ.
