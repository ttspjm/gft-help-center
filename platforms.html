Which platforms can I trade on?

Currently, our platform exclusively grants access to Ctrader, Tradelocker, MatchTrader, Volumetrica and MT5.

Please note that MT5 and Ctrader are not available to clients located in the USA.
All other platforms (TradeLocker, MatchTrader, and Volumetrica) are available to US traders.

Can I change my trading platform?

You may request one platform change, provided no trades have been placed on the account. Platform availability restrictions still apply.

Please contact our Support Team to request the change.

What is the account leverage?

The account leverage is the ratio of a trader’s capital to the size of positions they can control.

  • Forex 1:100
  • Indices : 1:20
  • Commodities : 1:20
  • Crypto : 1:2
  • Stocks : 1:5

Max Leverage is applied to all phases of the Evaluation.

Consequently, we use lower leverage to protect us from unforeseen losses as a distributor of large capital on a funded account.

  • Forex: 1:50
  • Indices : 1:10
  • Commodities : 1:10
  • Crypto : 1:2
  • Stocks : 1:5

Depending on the traders risk management we increase the leverage back to its Max.

How does the commission work for different trading instruments?

For MT5, Match-Trade and TradeLocker, our commission structure involves a flat fee of $0 per lot for cryptocurrencies, indices, commodities and stocks, $5 for FX pairs and metals with raw spread on all assets.

Do you offer Islamic or Swap-free accounts?

We do not offer Islamic or Swap-free accounts at this time. All challenge and funded accounts are subject to swap fees when trades are held overnight.
